# EagleCast brand system

## Visual direction
- Calm, premium, and editorial rather than neon or futuristic.
- Favor warm neutral surfaces, light backgrounds, and clear blue/green accents.
- Use generous whitespace, restrained motion, and confident typography.

## Palette
- Primary: #183a5b
- Secondary: #2f7d61
- Accent: #2f74ff
- Surface: #ffffff
- Page background: #f6f2e9
- Border: #e6dfd2

## Typography
- Headings: Manrope
- Body: Inter

## Layout principles
- Keep each section visually distinct.
- Use fewer cards and more editorial layouts with breathing room.
- Make trust, value, and clarity feel obvious at every step.
