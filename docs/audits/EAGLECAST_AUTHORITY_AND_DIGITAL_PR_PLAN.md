## Operating rules

1. Obtain backlink/referring-domain and competitor-overlap exports before selecting targets.
2. Score prospects for topical relevance, editorial standards, audience fit, traffic quality, and legitimate relationship—not authority alone.
3. Use precise, license-approved identity wording. Never use the brand owner’s profiles, reviews, or applications as this site’s assets.
4. No paid link schemes, automated placements, PBNs, fake reviews, or outreach without owner approval.
5. Track referring domain, source URL, anchor/context, target URL, follow/nofollow, first/last seen, referral traffic, and removals. Correlate with GSC trends; do not promise causation or rankings.

## First owner decisions

- Approve license scope and public relationship wording.
- Confirm business facts for citations and outreach.
- Approve which existing resources may be promoted.
- Approve spokesperson, outreach process, and review-collection policy.
