# EagleCast visual direction

## Brand personality
- Human, trustworthy, calm, premium, and commercially clear.
- Friendly without feeling playful or juvenile.
- Designed to feel like a serious consumer brand rather than a generic SaaS template.

## Color system
- Background: warm cream and soft ivory.
- Surface: white and light beige.
- Primary text: deep navy.
- Accent: calm blue.
- Secondary accent: fresh green.
- Highlight: soft coral or warm gold for calls to action.

## Typography
- Inter for body copy.
- Manrope for headings and product labels.
- Strong hierarchy, generous whitespace, and clear contrast across all breakpoints.

## Layout principles
- Editorial rhythm that alternates between split layouts, feature rows, and focused callouts.
- Every section should feel intentional and commercially useful.
- Minimal motion, polished spacing, and a calm conversion flow.
