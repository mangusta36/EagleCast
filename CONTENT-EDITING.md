# Content editing guide

- Main copy lives in the data folder under `data/`.
- Update navigation labels in `data/navigation.ts`.
- Update pricing plans in `data/pricing.ts`.
- Update FAQs in `data/faqs.ts`.
- Update blog and tutorial posts in `data/blog.ts` and `data/tutorials.ts`.
- Update support contact details in `data/contact.ts`.
- Legal pages should remain aligned with the verified EagleCast source content and should be reviewed before publishing.
